Layer 1 • Input & Output

Amazon Bedrock Guardrails

PROMPT_ATTACK (HIGH) and Denied Topic UnauthorizedOfferOrSalary block direct jailbreaks pre-harness; output filters anonymize EMAIL and PHONE PII.

Layer 2 • Blast Radius

Scoped IAM Explicit Deny

Execution role allows dynamodb:GetItem only on HireGuard_Candidates and attaches an explicit Deny on HireGuard_Offers, forcing AccessDeniedException.

Layer 3 • Server-Side Authority

Deterministic Lambda Policy Gate

HireGuardTools ignores any model- or prompt-supplied rubric_score, queries DynamoDB directly, and hardcodes db_score < 75 rejection in Python.

Layer 4 • Pre-Execution Gate & Vault

Human Approval + AgentCore Identity

Pre-execution policy middleware pauses irreversible rejections (APPROVAL_REQUIRED) pending recruiter sign-off. Model keys live in AWS AgentCore Identity.
